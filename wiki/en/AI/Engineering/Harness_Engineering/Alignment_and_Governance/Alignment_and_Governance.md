# Alignment and Governance

## Overview

**Alignment and Governance** goes beyond the defense and evaluation of individual request units to address whether the model itself behaves as intended and how organizations and society manage the associated risks. Alignment research studies model-level failure modes such as reward hacking and alignment faking, while mechanistic interpretability aims to explain the causes by looking directly into the model's internal circuits. Governance and compliance institutionalize these risks through frontier safety frameworks, standards, and regulations.
